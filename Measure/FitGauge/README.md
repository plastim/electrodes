# PlaStim Fit Gauge v2.2

A printed sizing ring. Slide it over the shaft and push each of its seven pins in until its pad touches. The seven
readings, together with the V-saddle at the top of the ring, give the shape of the cross-section, not just the girth.
The pins click every 1.25 mm.

## Folders

- `two-color/`: each part is one STEP with two bodies (the part + a 1.0 mm top layer), for a two-color print. In Bambu
  Studio the two parts only show when the Process row is switched from Global to Objects.
- `one-color/`: single solids with the same marks cut deeper (1.5 mm on the pins, 1.4 mm on the ring), for a
  single-color printer.

Each pin carries its own number (1 to 7) at its tail end and belongs in the hole with the same number. Pins 1 and 7,
2 and 6, 3 and 5 are the same length and differ only in that number.

Print order:

1. Pin 4 first, as a check. It is the longest pin and carries every kind of mark. The digits should read cleanly.
2. The ring. Once it is printed, try pin 4 in hole 4: it should click at every 1.25 mm.
3. The other six pins.

Assemble: each pin goes into the hole with its own number, tip first, marks up (the same side as the ring's marks).
Push it in from the outer end of its boss, under the arrow on the roof, toward the centre of the ring until it clicks.
The T head at the tail stops it at the last seat. Seen from the marked face, hole 1 is on the left.

## Reading

- Read at the pointer. On every boss an arrow on the roof points at a thin lip that lies on the pin. A mark is "at
  the pointer" when it sits against that lip: the last mark showing before the pin goes under the arrow.
- A line across the pin is a 10; the digit standing before it (toward the tip) says which: 1 = 10, 2 = 20, 3 = 30.
- A short tick on one edge is a 5 (5, 15, 25, 35). There are no other marks.
- The pin clicks every 1.25 mm, four clicks to every 5 mm. A line or a tick exactly at the pointer: the reading is
  that mark. One, two or three clicks further in than a mark: add 1.25, 2.5 or 3.75. The fourth click is the next
  mark. Example: the tick after the "1" line is at the pointer and the pin is two clicks past it: 15 + 2.5 = 17.5.
- The first line of all, the one with no digit, is the parking line: it stands for 0. Pull a pin out until the
  parking line is against the pointer and it clicks there, held, with its tip clear of the opening.
- The big number at the tail end is the pin's own number (1 to 7), not a reading.
- Wear it with the marks toward you, so the relieved side (the plain, unmarked face) is toward the head. Park all
  pins, slide the ring on, then push each pin in until its pad just touches, and read all seven.
